About GPA & CGPA Calculator

Welcome to GPA & CGPA Calculator—a lightweight, reliable, and instant academic tool designed specifically for university and college students worldwide.

Our Mission

We created this web tool to eliminate the confusion and tedious math involved in computing semester GPAs and cumulative CGPAs. Whether your institution uses a 5.0 Grading Scale or a 4.0 Grading Scale, our calculator gives you instant, accurate results along with your official degree class standing.

Why Use Our Tool?

100% Free, Mobile-Friendly, Privacy-Focused (No login required, data stays strictly in your browser), and Fast.
